Mod Guide
📌 Pinning Messages
Click Pin on any message to display it on stream in the chat pin overlay. Only one message can be pinned at a time. Click Clear to remove it.
👤 Username Panel
Click any username to open a panel showing all their messages this session, their mod history, and known aliases.
🔴 Mod Actions
Click Mod on any message to open the action panel. Select a reason, add optional notes, then choose Delete, Timeout, or Ban. Bans require confirmation. All actions are logged with your name.
⭐ Hall of Fame
Save memorable messages permanently via the Mod panel. Viewable in the Hall of Fame tab.
🗑 Review Queue
Messages containing blacklisted words are auto-deleted and appear here with surrounding context. Review and take further action if needed.
⚠ Keyword Flags
Greylist words highlight a message yellow — no action taken. Blacklist words highlight red and auto-delete. Manage both lists in Settings.
🆕 New Account Badge
Accounts newer than the threshold (default 30 days) show a yellow NEW badge with the account age. Useful for spotting alt accounts.
🐢 Slow Mode
Toggle slow mode from the stream bar or Settings. Set the delay between messages per user.
📊 Polls
Start polls from saved templates or create a new one. The slider overlay updates live as votes come in. End a poll from the Polls tab.
🤖 Auto-timeout (Bot)
If a user pastes the same message 3+ times in 60 seconds, the bot automatically times them out and logs it. Configure in Settings.
